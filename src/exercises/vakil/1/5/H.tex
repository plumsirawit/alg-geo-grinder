EXERCISE. Suppose $M$ is a finitely presented $A$-module: $M$ has a finite number of generators, and with these generators it has a finite number of relations; or, equivalently, and usefully, fits in an exact sequence (1.5.9.1) $A^{\oplus q} \to A^{\oplus p} \to M \to 0$. Use (1.5.9.1) and the left-exactness of $\operatorname{Hom}$ to describe an isomorphism $S^{-1}\operatorname{Hom}_A(M,N) \cong \operatorname{Hom}_{S^{-1}A}(S^{-1}M,S^{-1}N)$. (You might be able to interpret this in light of a variant of Exercise 1.5.I below, for left-exact contravariant functors rather than right-exact covariant functors.)
