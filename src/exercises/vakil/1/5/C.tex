IMPORTANT EXERCISE. Suppose $\mathcal{C}$ is an abelian category. Define the category $\operatorname{Com}_{\mathcal{C}}$ of complexes as follows. The objects are infinite complexes $A^\bullet: \cdots\to A_{i-1}\xrightarrow{f_{i-1}}A_i\xrightarrow{f_i}A_{i+1}\xrightarrow{f_{i+1}}\cdots$ in $\mathcal{C}$, and the morphisms $A^\bullet\to B^\bullet$ are commuting diagrams
$$\begin{array}{ccccccccc} \cdots&\to&A_{i-1}&\xrightarrow{f_{i-1}}&A_i&\xrightarrow{f_i}&A_{i+1}&\to&\cdots \\ &&\downarrow g_{i-1}&&\downarrow g_i&&\downarrow g_{i+1} \\ \cdots&\to&B_{i-1}&\xrightarrow{g_{i-1}}&B_i&\xrightarrow{g_i}&B_{i+1}&\to&\cdots \end{array}$$
Show that $\operatorname{Com}_{\mathcal{C}}$ is an abelian category.
