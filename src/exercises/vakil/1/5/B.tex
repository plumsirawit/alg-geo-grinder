EXERCISE AND IMPORTANT DEFINITION. Suppose $0\to A_1\to\cdots\to A_n\to0$ is a complex of finite-dimensional $k$-vector spaces (often called $A^\bullet$ for short). Define $h_i(A^\bullet):=\dim H_i(A^\bullet)$. Show that
$$\sum_i(-1)^i\dim A_i=\sum_i(-1)^i h_i(A^\bullet).$$
In particular, if $A^\bullet$ is exact, then $\sum_i(-1)^i\dim A_i=0$. (If you have not dealt much with cohomology, this will give you some practice.)
