EXERCISE. Suppose
$$\begin{array}{ccccccccc} \cdots&\to&A_{n+1}&\to&B_{n+1}&\to&C_{n+1}&\to&0 \\ &&\downarrow&&\downarrow&&\downarrow \\ 0&\to&A_n&\to&B_n&\to&C_n&\to&0 \\ &&\downarrow&&\downarrow&&\downarrow \\ \cdots&\to&A_0&\to&B_0&\to&C_0&\to&0 \end{array}$$
is an inverse system of exact sequences of modules over a ring, such that the maps $A_{n+1}\to A_n$ are surjective. (We say: "transition maps of the left term are surjective.") Show that the limit
$$0\to\varprojlim A_n\to\varprojlim B_n\to\varprojlim C_n\to0$$
is also exact. (You will need to define the maps in the displayed sequence.)
