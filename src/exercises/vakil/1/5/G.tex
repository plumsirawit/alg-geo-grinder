EXERCISE. Suppose $A$ is a ring, $S\subset A$ is a multiplicative subset, and $M$ is an $A$-module. (a) Show that localization of $A$-modules $\operatorname{Mod}_A\to\operatorname{Mod}_{S^{-1}A}$ is an exact covariant functor. (b) Show that $(-)\otimes_A M$ is a right-exact covariant functor $\operatorname{Mod}_A\to\operatorname{Mod}_A$. (This is a repeat of Exercise 1.2.H.) (c) Show that $\operatorname{Hom}_A(M,-)$ is a left-exact covariant functor $\operatorname{Mod}_A\to\operatorname{Mod}_A$. If $\mathcal{C}$ is any abelian category, and $C\in\mathcal{C}$, show that $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(C,-)$ is a left-exact covariant functor $\mathcal{C}\to\mathrm{Ab}$. (d) Show that $\operatorname{Hom}_A(-,M)$ is a left-exact contravariant functor $\operatorname{Mod}_A\to\operatorname{Mod}_A$. If $\mathcal{C}$ is any abelian category, and $C\in\mathcal{C}$, show that $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,C)$ is a left-exact contravariant functor $\mathcal{C}\to\mathrm{Ab}$.
