UNIMPORTANT EXERCISE (GRAFTING EXACT SEQUENCES, A VARIANT OF THE SNAKE LEMMA). Extend the Snake Lemma as follows. Suppose we have a commuting diagram
$$
\begin{array}{ccccccccc}
0 & \to & X' & \to & Y' & \to & Z' & \to & A' & \to \cdots \\
  &     & \downarrow a & & \downarrow b & & \downarrow c \\
\cdots & \to & W & \to & X & \to & Y & \to & Z & \to 0
\end{array}
$$
where the top and bottom rows are exact. Show that the top and bottom rows can be “grafted together” to an exact sequence
$$
\cdots \to W \to \ker a \to \ker b \to \ker c \to \operatorname{coker} a \to \operatorname{coker} b \to \operatorname{coker} c \to A' \to \cdots.
$$
