EXERCISE. Show that $\phi : M \to S^{-1}M$ exists, by constructing something satisfying the universal property. Hint: Define elements of $S^{-1}M$ to be of the form $m/s$ where $m \in M$ and $s \in S$, and
$$
\frac{m_1}{s_1} = \frac{m_2}{s_2}
\quad\text{if and only if}\quad
\text{for some }s\in S,\ s(s_2m_1-s_1m_2)=0.
$$
Define the additive structure by
$$
\frac{m_1}{s_1}+\frac{m_2}{s_2}=\frac{s_2m_1+s_1m_2}{s_1s_2},
$$
and the $S^{-1}A$-module structure (and hence the $A$-module structure) as given by
$$
\frac{a_1}{s_1}\cdot\frac{m_2}{s_2}=\frac{a_1m_2}{s_1s_2}.
$$
