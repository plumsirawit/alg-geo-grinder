EXERCISE. If $Z$ is the final object in a category $\mathcal{C}$, and $X,Y\in\mathcal{C}$, show that
$$X\times_ZY\cong X\times Y:$$
“the” fibered product over $Z$ is uniquely isomorphic to “the” product. Assume all relevant (fibered) products exist. (This is an exercise about unwinding the definition.)
