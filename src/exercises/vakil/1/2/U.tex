EXERCISE. Suppose $A\to B$ and $A\to C$ are two ring morphisms, so in particular $B$ and $C$ are $A$-modules. Recall (Exercise 1.2.K) that $B\otimes_A C$ has a ring structure. Show that there is a natural morphism $B\to B\otimes_A C$ given by $b\mapsto b\otimes1$. (This is not necessarily an inclusion; see Exercise 1.2.G.) Similarly, there is a natural morphism $C\to B\otimes_A C$. Show that this gives a fibered coproduct on rings, i.e., that
$$
\begin{array}{ccc}
B\otimes_A C &\longleftarrow& C\\
\uparrow && \uparrow\\
B &\longleftarrow& A
\end{array}
$$
satisfies the universal property of fibered coproduct.
