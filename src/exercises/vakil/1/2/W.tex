EXERCISE. Prove that a morphism $\pi:X\to Y$ is a monomorphism if and only if the fibered product $X\times_YX$ exists, and the induced diagonal morphism $\delta_\pi:X\to X\times_YX$ (Definition 1.2.7) is an isomorphism. We may then take this as the definition of monomorphism. (Monomorphisms aren’t central to future discussions, although they will come up again. This exercise is just good practice.)
