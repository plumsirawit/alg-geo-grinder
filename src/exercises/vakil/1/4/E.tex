EXERCISE. Suppose $B\to A$ is a morphism of rings. If $M$ is an $A$-module, you can create a $B$-module $M_B$ by considering it as a $B$-module. This gives a functor $(-)_B:\operatorname{Mod}_A\to\operatorname{Mod}_B$. Show that this functor is right-adjoint to $-\otimes_B A$. In other words, describe a bijection
$$\operatorname{Hom}_A(N\otimes_B A,M)\cong\operatorname{Hom}_B(N,M_B)$$
functorial in both arguments. (This adjoint pair is very important.)
