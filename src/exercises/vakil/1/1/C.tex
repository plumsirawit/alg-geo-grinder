EXERCISE. Let $(\cdot)^{\vee\vee} : \mathrm{f.d.}\,\mathrm{Vec}_k \to \mathrm{f.d.}\,\mathrm{Vec}_k$ be the double dual functor from the category of finite-dimensional vector spaces over $k$ to itself. Show that $(\cdot)^{\vee\vee}$ is naturally isomorphic to the identity functor on $\mathrm{f.d.}\,\mathrm{Vec}_k$. (Without the finite-dimensionality hypothesis, we only get a natural transformation of functors from $\operatorname{id}$ to $(\cdot)^{\vee\vee}$.)
