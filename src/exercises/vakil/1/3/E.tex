EXERCISE. Suppose $I$ is filtered. (We will almost exclusively use the case where $I$ is a filtered set.) Recall the symbol $\coprod$ for disjoint union of sets. Show that any diagram in $\mathrm{Sets}$ indexed by $I$ has the following, with the obvious maps to it, as a colimit:
$$\left(\coprod_{i\in I}A_i\right)/{\sim},$$
where $(a_i,i)\sim(a_j,j)$ if and only if there are $f:A_i\to A_k$ and $g:A_j\to A_k$ in the diagram for which $f(a_i)=g(a_j)$ in $A_k$. (You will see that the “filtered” hypothesis is there to ensure that $\sim$ is an equivalence relation.)
