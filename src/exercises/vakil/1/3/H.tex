EXERCISE: COLIMITS OF $A$-MODULES WITHOUT THE FILTERED CONDITION. Suppose you are given a diagram of $A$-modules indexed by $I$: $F:I\to\operatorname{Mod}_A$, where we let $M_i:=F(i)$. Show that the colimit is
$$\bigoplus_{i\in I}M_i$$
modulo the relations $m_i-F(n)(m_i)$ for every $n:i\to j$ in $I$ (i.e., for every arrow in the diagram). (Somewhat more precisely: “modulo” means “quotiented by the submodule generated by.”)
