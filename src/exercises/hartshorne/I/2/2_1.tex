Prove the "homogeneous Nullstellensatz," which says if $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq S$ is a homogeneous ideal, and if $f \in S$ is a homogeneous polynomial with $\deg f > 0$, such that $f(P) = 0$ for all $P \in Z(\mathfrak{a})$ in $\mathbb{P}^n$, then $f^q \in \mathfrak{a}$ for some $q > 0$. [Hint: Interpret the problem in terms of the affine $(n+1)$-space whose affine coordinate ring is $S$, and use the usual Nullstellensatz, (1.3A).]
